Terms of Service

Last updated: 5 August 2026

Draw-tionary is a free drawing game for Discord, run by one person as a hobby project. These are the rules for using it.

1. Agreeing to these terms

By adding Draw-tionary to a Discord server, or by playing it, you agree to these terms. If you don't agree, don't use it — remove the bot or stop running /draw.

You must also follow Discord's Terms of Service and Community Guidelines. Draw-tionary runs inside Discord and doesn't override anything they require.

2. Who can use it

You must meet Discord's minimum age requirement — 13, or higher where local law says so. To add the bot to a server you need the Manage Server permission there.

3. What you draw

Your drawings are yours. You keep ownership of anything you make. By posting a drawing, you grant permission to store it and display it to other members of the server you posted it in, which is simply what makes the game work.

You may delete your own drawings at any time, without asking anyone.

4. Acceptable use

Don't draw or submit:

  • Sexual content involving minors, in any form. This is the one rule with no discussion attached — accounts are removed and reports are made to the appropriate authorities.
  • Sexually explicit content, gore, or shock imagery.
  • Hate symbols, or content attacking people for their race, ethnicity, religion, disability, sex, gender identity, or sexual orientation.
  • Harassment of a specific person, or content designed to intimidate.
  • Threats of violence, or encouragement of self-harm.
  • Anyone's private information — addresses, phone numbers, or anything else that identifies a person who didn't share it.
  • Content that breaks the law where you or your server's members live.

Also, don't:

  • Try to break, overload, or probe the service for vulnerabilities. (If you find one, please report it — see below.)
  • Automate play, script the API, or manufacture points.
  • Attempt to see a word you haven't earned, or another player's data.

5. Moderation

Every drawing can be reported by any player. Two reports hide a drawing automatically, pending review. Server moderators — anyone with Manage Messages — can remove any drawing in their server.

Content may also be removed, and access blocked, without notice where something clearly breaks section 4. Reports are anonymous: the person who drew it is never told who reported them.

Moderation within a server is that server's responsibility. Draw-tionary provides the tools; it does not police individual communities.

6. Availability

This is a hobby project offered free of charge. There is no uptime guarantee, no support commitment, and no promise that it will keep running. It may be changed, interrupted, or discontinued at any time.

Please keep your own copy of anything you'd be sad to lose — the canvas has a Save PNG option for exactly this reason.

7. Disclaimer and liability

Draw-tionary is provided "as is", without warranty of any kind, express or implied, including warranties of merchantability, fitness for a particular purpose, and non-infringement.

To the fullest extent the law allows, the operator is not liable for any indirect, incidental, or consequential damages arising from your use of the service, including lost drawings, lost scores, or service downtime.

Some jurisdictions don't allow these exclusions, in which case they apply to you only as far as local law permits.

8. Reporting a security problem

Please report vulnerabilities privately, through GitHub's private vulnerability reporting, rather than in a public issue or by demonstrating it on other players. Good-faith reports are welcome and will not be pursued.

A public issue would tell everyone how to exploit the problem before there's a fix, which is why this one channel is deliberately private.

9. Changes to these terms

These terms may change. The date at the top will be updated when they do, and continuing to use Draw-tionary after a change means accepting the revised version.

10. Contact

Questions, bugs, and feature requests: open an issue on GitHub.

Data requests — deletion, or a copy of what's stored about you — go to privacy@draw-tionary.app, because those shouldn't be public. See the privacy policy.

Note. These terms were written to be honest and readable rather than exhaustive, and are not legal advice. Before relying on them — especially the liability and moderation sections — have a lawyer review them for the jurisdictions your players are actually in.
