Pictionary for friends who are never online at the same time.

How a round goes

Five steps, none of them timed.

  1. Ask for a word

    Run /draw. You get three words — one easy, one medium, one hard — and a private link only you can open.

  2. Draw it

    Pencil, finger, or mouse, all welcome. There's no timer, so take four seconds or take an hour.

  3. Post it

    Your drawing appears in the channel with the word hidden behind a row of empty boxes.

  4. Everyone guesses

    They can watch the whole thing being drawn, stroke by stroke, which is usually funnier than the finished picture.

  5. Points go out

    To whoever guesses it — and to you, for drawing something people could actually work out.

Three words, three difficulties

Harder words are worth more. Pick the hard one and fail gloriously — nothing is deducted for a drawing nobody gets.

  • Easy
    10 pts

    125 words

  • Medium
    20 pts

    100 words

  • Hard
    35 pts

    70 words

Guess early and you'll earn a bonus on top — up to half the word's value again. The window is generous (30 seconds for easy, a full minute for hard) and re-watching a drawing costs nothing, so being fast is a bonus rather than the whole game.

Things we decided on purpose

  • Wrong guesses cost nothing

    Guess as many times as you like. The failure mode worth worrying about is people not joining in, not people guessing badly.

  • No timer, anywhere

    Not on drawing, not on guessing. The countdown is the part of Pictionary that keeps quiet people quiet.

  • All 124 colors, free

    No unlocking, no points to spend, no premium palette. They're just there, for everyone, from the first round.

  • Pressure-sensitive if your device is

    Apple Pencil and drawing tablets get variable stroke width. A finger on a phone gets a clean constant line.

  • Your drawings stay

    The thing you spent an hour on doesn't expire. Go back and watch it again whenever you want.

  • Nobody watches you draw

    No live spectating, no "3 people are watching." You draw alone and share when you're ready.

Adding it to your server

  1. Press Add to Discord and pick your server. You'll need Manage Server permission there.
  2. Approve the two permissions it asks for — that's the whole list.
  3. Run /draw in any channel you want to play in. That's it; there's nothing to configure.

It asks for very little

Draw-tionary needs exactly two permissions — Send Messages and Embed Links — in the channels you play in.

It cannot read your message history, see your member list, or read anything you type outside of a guess you deliberately submit. It doesn't hold a live connection to your server at all.

Commands

/draw — get a word and a canvas.
/scores — see who's ahead in this server.

Keeping it friendly

This was built for a small community, so moderation came first rather than after something went wrong.

  • Anyone can report

    Every drawing carries a report button, and reports are anonymous — the drawer never learns who flagged them.

  • Two reports hide it

    A drawing with two reports hides itself pending review. No single person can hide someone's work on their own.

  • Moderators can remove anything

    Anyone with Manage Messages in your server can take a drawing down for good.

  • Delete your own work

    Your drawing is yours. You can remove it whenever you like, without asking anyone.

Scores are per-server, and drawings never travel between servers. See the privacy policy for exactly what's stored.
