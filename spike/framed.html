Draw-tionary — iframe test harness

This embeds the drawing spike inside an iframe at roughly the proportions Discord gives an Activity. If pointer input behaves the same here as it does when you open draw.html directly, the Activity route is safe.

Draw-tionary · Activity
What to check on the iPad:
  1. Diagnostics should show In iframe: YES.
  2. Draw with the Pencil — Type should read pen.
  3. Vary your pressure mid-stroke — Pressure should move off 0.5 and the stroke should visibly thicken and thin.
  4. Coalesced above 1 means you are getting the full input rate, not a downsampled one.
  5. Try to scroll the page with a finger while drawing — it should not scroll.
  6. Compare all of the above against Open unframed.
